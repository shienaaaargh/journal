## 🟣 Today in one sentence
Spark is an engine; SQL is a language, and a green pipeline doesn't inherently mean good data.

## 🧠 What I learned
* **A lot.** I realized Spark is designed for scaling across machines, not just handling more rows on a single machine.
* **Pipeline success ≠ Data Quality.** Just because the code executed successfully doesn't mean the resulting metrics are accurate.
* **The 6 DQ checks to catch bad data:** Nulls, Unique, Range, Accepted Values, Referential Integrity, and Volume.
* We should be writing DQ results into a dedicated table (`dq_check_results`) instead of relying on one-off query checks.

## 🔮 Terms I am still learning
* **Shuffle:** Moving data between partitions in Spark (which can quickly become computationally expensive).
* **Checkpointed Pipeline:** Adding explicit "CHECK" stages so bad data is halted before it flows into the final dashboard though we have been adding these to our Week 4 exercises!

## 🤔 What confused me
* How to fully automate the `dq_check_results` table pipeline for all of our active datasets.
